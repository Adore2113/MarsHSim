#### Primary Battery Room:
    ♡ shape: rectangle
    ♡ floor area: ~ 110 m²
    ♡ width: ~ 11.0 m
    ♡ height: ~ 4.5 m
    ♡ volume: ~ 495 m³
    ♡ calculation:
        - ~ 11.0 m × 10.0 m = 110 m²
        - ~ 110 m² × 4.5 m = 495 m³

    ♡ minimum maintenance aisle width: ~ 1.2 m

    ♡ access / placement:
        - entrance into the Power Distribution and Transfer Room
        - does not open directly into the secondary battery corridor

#### Secondary Battery Bank and Corridor:
    ♡ 12 isolated battery rooms
    ♡ floor area of rooms: ~ 2,182 m²
    ♡ hallway: ~ 268 m²
    ♡ rooms and hallway together: ~ 2,450 m²
    ♡ height: ~ 5.5 m
    ♡ each room: ~ 1,000 m³
    ♡ room volume: ~ 12,000 m³
    ♡ hallway volume: ~ 1,475 m³
    ♡ rooms and hallway together: ~ 13,475 m³

    ♡ calculation:
        - 1,000 m³ ÷ 5.5 m = ~ 181.8 m²/room
        - 181.8 m² × 12 = ~ 2,182 m²
        - 12 × 1,000 m³ = 12,000 m³
        - 2,182 + 268 = 2,450 m²
        - 2,450 m² × 5.5 m = 13,475 m³

    ♡ access / placement:
        - central hallway with 6 rooms along each side
        
        - one end connects to the Power Distribution and Transfer Room
        
        - other end connects to the Battery Maintenance and Service Room
        
        - emergency exit through the service room

    ♡ includes:
        - 12 isolated battery rooms
        - central hallway

    ♡ used for:
        - bulk stored energy
        - isolating one room for maintenance or emergencies

    ♡ lower access priority

    ♡ the 268 m² hallway is this corridor, not an extra hub hallway

### Design Decisions:
#### Why is this hub so much larger than the water rooms?
    ♡ the secondary side is 12 isolated rooms plus a hallway

    ♡ it's bigger than the original 2,000 m³ habitat idea because of how much stored energy it holds

#### Why not open the primary room straight into the corridor?
    ♡ distribution sits in the middle

    ♡ a problem in the corridor doesn't go straight into the everyday battery room

#### Why not add another corridor on top of 2,750 m²?
    ♡ the 268 m² is already the walking space for the 12 rooms

    ♡ the connection from utility is already counted in the utility 150 m²
