# Power / Energy Hub Layout Plans
### General Notes:
    ♡ preliminary estimates

    ♡ floor areas below are the rooms themselves unless marked otherwise

    ♡ equipment volume is included within its room and isn't added separately

    ♡ every room is assumed to have sensors

    ♡ the Power / Energy Hub is next to the Utility / Resource Hub and is calculated separately

    ♡ the secondary battery hallway is the 268 m² corridor

## Arcadia Power / Energy Hub (updated 09/14/2026):
    ♡ room area: ~ 2,482 m²
    ♡ corridor area: ~ 268 m²
    ♡ rooms and corridor together: ~ 2,750 m²
    ♡ room volume: ~ 13,470 m³
    ♡ corridor volume: ~ 1,475 m³
    ♡ rooms and corridor volume together: ~ 14,945 m³

    ♡ calculation:
        - 110 + 2,182 + 70 + 120
          = 2,482 m² rooms

        - 2,482 + 268
          = 2,750 m² together

        - 495 + 12,000 + 315 + 660
          = 13,470 m³ rooms

        - 13,470 + 1,475
          = 14,945 m³ together

    ♡ contains:
        - Primary Battery Room
        - Secondary Battery Bank and Corridor
        - Power Distribution and Transfer Room
        - Battery Maintenance and Service Room

#### Primary Battery Room:
    ♡ shape: rectangle
    ♡ floor area: ~ 110 m²
    ♡ width: ~ 11.0 m
    ♡ height: ~ 4.5 m
    ♡ volume: ~ 495 m³
    ♡ calculation:
        - ~ 11.0 m × 10.0 m = 110 m²
        - ~ 110 m² × 4.5 m = 495 m³

    ♡ minimum maintenance aisle width: ~ 1.2 m

    ♡ access / placement:
        - entrance into the Power Distribution and Transfer Room
        - does not open directly into the secondary battery corridor

    ♡ includes:
        - 4 separate battery banks
        - capacity per bank: ~ 6,250 kWh
        - banks arranged symmetrically
        - central maintenance and access area

    ♡ used for:
        - everyday battery storage closest to distribution

#### Secondary Battery Bank and Corridor:
    ♡ 12 isolated battery rooms
    ♡ floor area of rooms: ~ 2,182 m²
    ♡ hallway: ~ 268 m²
    ♡ rooms and hallway together: ~ 2,450 m²
    ♡ height: ~ 5.5 m
    ♡ each room: ~ 1,000 m³
    ♡ room volume: ~ 12,000 m³
    ♡ hallway volume: ~ 1,475 m³
    ♡ rooms and hallway together: ~ 13,475 m³

    ♡ calculation:
        - 1,000 m³ ÷ 5.5 m = ~ 181.8 m²/room
        - 181.8 m² × 12 = ~ 2,182 m²
        - 12 × 1,000 m³ = 12,000 m³
        - 2,182 + 268 = 2,450 m²
        - 2,450 m² × 5.5 m = 13,475 m³

    ♡ access / placement:
        - central hallway with 6 rooms along each side
        
        - one end connects to the Power Distribution and Transfer Room
        
        - other end connects to the Battery Maintenance and Service Room
        
        - emergency exit through the service room

    ♡ includes:
        - 12 isolated battery rooms
        - central hallway

    ♡ used for:
        - bulk stored energy
        - isolating one room for maintenance or emergencies

    ♡ lower access priority

    ♡ the 268 m² hallway is this corridor, not an extra hub hallway

    ♡ width: ~ 10.0 m

    ♡ access / placement:

    ♡ includes:

    ♡ used for:
        - moving power between battery rooms and the habitat

    ♡ height: ~ 5.5 m
    ♡ volume: ~ 660 m³
    ♡ calculation:

    ♡ access / placement:
