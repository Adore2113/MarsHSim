# Structural Zone Overview
### General Notes:
    ♡ zones can seal independently for redundancy and quarantine

    ♡ uses averaged plant behavior

    ♡ plant uptake and operational losses reduce reservoir water

    ♡ reservoir receives make-up water when it reaches the refill threshold

    ♡ primary and backup pumps provide circulation redundancy

    ♡ supplemental LED power depends on sunlight and greenhouse power mode
    
    ♡ LED lighting contributes additional heat according to LED power use
### ----------------------------------------
## Zone Overviews:

### Structural
    ♡ zone role:
        - larger, longer cycle, bulk calorie crops
        - fat and protein crops that need more space and time

    ♡ crop considerations:
        - sweet potato
        - quinoa
        - peanuts
        - dwarf banana

    ♡ floor area: ~ 380 m²
    ♡ effective grow area: ~ 420 m²

    ♡ growing system:
        - LECA growing beds along the helix
        - beds divided into manageable modules
        - ~ 70 bed modules
        - ~ 6 m² effective grow area per bed module

### Hydroponics:
    ♡ method:
        - intermittent drip-fed LECA beds
        - shared structural zone reservoir

    ♡ circulation:
        1. reservoir
        2. pump
        3. drip irrigation
        4. LECA beds
        5. drainage
        6. reservoir

    ♡ pH target: ~ 5.8
    ♡ EC target: ~ 2.1
    ♡ solution temperature target: ~ 23°C
    ♡ dissolved oxygen: ≥ 6 mg/L

### Reservoir:
    ♡ tank capacity: ~ 7,000 L
    ♡ working volume: ~ 6,300 L
    ♡ normal level: ~ 5,950 L
    ♡ auto-refill: 4,900 L / 70%
    ♡ low: < 3,500 L / 50%
    ♡ critical: < 1,750 L / 25%

    ♡ make-up water:
        - supplied from shared treated habitat water
        - reservoir refills toward normal level

### Pumps:
    ♡ primary circulation pump: ~ 1.5 kW
    ♡ backup circulation pump: ~ 1.5 kW
    ♡ backup pump while offline / waiting: ~ 0.0 kW

    ♡ pump failure behavior:
        - backup pump automatically takes over
        - if both pumps fail, structural plant health declines more slowly than
          the other zones because the LECA beds retain moisture

### Lighting:
    ♡ photoperiod: 16 hours
    ♡ light period: 05:00–21:00 LMST
    ♡ zone light target: 0.26 kW/m²

    ♡ lighting:
        - hybrid sunlight + supplemental LED
        - LED demand changes with available sunlight

### Gas Exchange:
    ♡ light-period average CO₂ uptake rate:
        10 µmol CO₂/m²/s
        ~ 0.576 mol CO₂/m²/sol

    ♡ baseline light-period CO₂ uptake:
        ~ 241.92 mol CO₂/sol

    ♡ dark-period average CO₂ release rate:
        ~ 3.0 µmol CO₂/m²/s
        ~ 0.0864 mol CO₂/m²/sol

    ♡ baseline dark-period CO₂ release:
        ~ 36.29 mol CO₂/sol

    ♡ photosynthetic quotient (PQ): 1.03

    ♡ baseline light-period O₂ production:
        ~ 249.18 mol O₂/sol

    ♡ respiratory quotient (RQ): 0.90

    ♡ baseline dark-period O₂ consumption:
        ~ 40.32 mol O₂/sol

### Water:
    ♡ plant water uptake: ~ 2.5 kg/m²/sol
    ♡ baseline plant water uptake: ~ 1,050 kg/sol
    ♡ operational water loss allowance: ~ 4%
    ♡ baseline operational water loss: ~ 42.0 kg/sol

    ♡ plant water uptake contributes to:
        - transpiration
        - plant biomass

    ♡ greenhouse wide V1 water split:
        - ~ 95% transpiration
        - ~ 5% retained in plant biomass (V1 placeholder)

    ♡ transpired water:
        - enters greenhouse atmosphere
        - collected as condensate by CHX
        - sent to WPA for processing
        - recovered water returns to habitat water storage

### Power:
    ♡ hydroponic circulation pump:
        ~ 1.5 kW while operating

    ♡ supplemental LED power:
        depends on sunlight and greenhouse power mode

### Heat:
    ♡ plant / structural heat density: 0.015 kW/m²

    ♡ LED lighting contributes additional heat according to LED power use
