    ♡ panels/array: 45

    ♡ calculation:

    ♡ total control blocks: 50
    ♡ arrays/block: 45 
    ♡ panels/block: 2,025

        - 45 arrays × 45 panels = 2,025 panels/block
        - 89.9 m² × 45 ≈ 4,046 m²/block
